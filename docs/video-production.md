# Creative video production

Use up to six levels when decomposition buys real expertise; most runs need two. Do not create chains just to fill the limit. Only the main agent integrates shared files and starts the full render. Leads must actually dispatch distinct child sessions and report their IDs and artifacts. If nested dispatch fails, main dispatches the specialists itself. If no dispatch is possible, disclose it and do not label self-review independent.

1. **Start story, art exploration and stock search in parallel.** Give each lead the brief, confirmed facts/sources, privacy limits and run path. Each child gets one role, exclusive file ownership and one verifiable deliverable.
2. **Story lead (`video-planner`).** Dispatch `video-writer` for `out/script-draft.md`, then pass the draft to `video-script-editor` for `out/script-final.md`. Lock the hook, evidence, time budget, spoken copy and on-screen copy before timing final motion. Cut unsupported claims, job guarantees, filler and metric dumps. Use measured voice duration to finalize the beat sheet.
3. **Visual lead (`video-visual-lead`).** Dispatch `video-art-director` for two different subject-specific treatments and a selected direction in `out/art-direction.md`. Once story and direction are ready, dispatch `video-motion-designer` for a frame-by-frame signature sequence and prototype. Review the prototype before assigning separate scenes to `video-builder`. Build shots around real evidence (such as an authorized product capture, repository artifact or precise demonstration), not dashboards with swapped labels. Pulled focus, a match cut or a reveal must change what the viewer understands. Glow, count-ups, radar rings and fade-ups do not count by themselves. Both specialists must load `frontend-design`; motion designers and builders also load `remotion-best-practices`.
4. **Sound lead (`video-sound-lead`).** Dispatch `video-stock-scout` during concept work, then `video-builder` as voice/mix engineer once script and sources are ready. Use actual license-verified stock by default for creative work that needs a music bed, unless the user specifies silence or another source. Follow the [audio workflow](audio-workflow.md); record search attempts, chosen files and credits in `out/`. If stock is requested and no eligible original is available, ask for a supplied asset or approval of a different direction. Never silently substitute a generated tone or a catalog preview. Record the actual mix input and match it to the credit.
5. **Review before release.** Give `video-reviewer` the draft and final scripts, design plan, motion prototype, stock credits, exact mix inputs and integrated preview. It assigns separate picture/motion and audio/provenance checks to fresh `video-quality-checker` sessions. Require timestamped frame evidence and inspection of the complete signature transition in motion, plus listening or an explicit limitation. If privacy blocks inspection, ask for local user review and mark that gate pending. Reject generic visual treatment, motion without narrative purpose, fabricated claims or missing required stock. Return findings to the original owner and retest. After the creative gates, render once and verify the exported streams and full decode. Report any pending gate; passing decode does not clear creative review.
